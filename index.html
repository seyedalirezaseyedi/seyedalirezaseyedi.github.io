Books & Monographs

Book / authored chapter collection · Nova Science Publishers

A Note on Inheritance and Generalizability Properties in Optimal Control Problems

Optimization & Optimal Control
Seyedalireza Seyedi, Iraj Sadegh Amiri and Sara Chaghervand
Nova Science Publishers · Mathematics Research Developments · 2016/2017 · Softcover ISBN 978-1-63485-784-0 · eBook ISBN 978-1-63485-796-3
Short abstract. The book develops the idea that structural properties defined for functions can be inherited or generalized to functionals, introducing abstract-convex, convex-along-rays and increasing positively homogeneous functionals. It then investigates how the global-search Cutting Angle Method can be extended from functions to variational and optimal-control problems, including a Unit Vectors Combinations Technique and combination of CAM with the DISOPE local-search algorithm.

  1. Abstract Convex Functionals Defined on Rⁿ and their Subdifferentiability
  2. Convex-Along-Rays Functionals Defined on Rⁿ and their Subdifferentiability
  3. Increasing Positively Homogeneous Functional Defined on Rⁿ and their Subdifferentiability
  4. The Cutting Angle Method and a Local Search on Optimal Control Problems
  5. The Cutting Angle Method for Solving of Variational Problems
  6. The Cutting Angle Method on Optimal Control Problems

The Numerical Solution of Continuous Time Optimal Control Problems with the Cutting Angle Method

Optimization & Optimal Control
Seyedalireza Seyedi, Iraj Sadegh Amiri, Sara Chaghervand and Volker J. Sorger
Nova Science Publishers · Mathematics Research Developments · 2018 · Softcover ISBN 978-1-53613-143-7 · eBook ISBN 978-1-53613-144-4
Short abstract. This monograph develops a global-search framework for continuous-time optimal-control problems using the Cutting Angle Method. It formalizes inheritance and generalizability from functions to functionals, studies new classes of functionals, and presents a generalized CAM suitable for optimal control. The final part combines CAM with a local-search method to improve convergence while retaining a mechanism for checking global optimality.

Chapters

  1. Introduction on Cutting Angle Method Inspired by Abstract Convexity for Solving Continuous Time Optimal Control Problems
  2. A Wide Literature Review on Building the Cutting Angle Method
  3. The Inheritance and Generalizability Properties Extended From Function Definitions into Functionals
  4. Study of Some New Type of Functionals Defined Based the Inheritance and Generalizability Properties of Functions
  5. Capability of Function Optimization Algorithms for Solving Optimal Control Problems With Respect to the Inheritance and Generalizability Properties
  6. A Generalized Version of Cutting Angle Method for Solving Continuous Time Optimal Control Problems
