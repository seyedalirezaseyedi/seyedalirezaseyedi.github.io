Research Project

Quantitative Water Governance Fit & Institutional Restructuring

A quantitative framework for evaluating how administrative water-governance structures align with the hydrological organization of the Urmia Lake Basin.

The model compares 1-core, 2-core, and 3-core governance configurations using a Curie–Weiss mean-field formulation. The principal outputs are interaction handling, Hamiltonian structural system cost, and zonal pressure (gain). This dashboard extends the published framework into a dynamic scenario-analysis environment.

Study area Urmia Lake Basin, Iran
Reference period 2005
Sectors Drinking · Agriculture · Industry
